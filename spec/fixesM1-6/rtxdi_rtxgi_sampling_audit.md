# RTXDI and RTXGI sampling audit

Reviewed 2026-11-20 against RTXDI 3.2 and the RTXGI 2.9 Indirect Diffuse sample. The engine pins RTXDI-Library at `b67f61c` and NRC at `7de30e4` (0.16). This audit covers ReSTIR DI, ReSTIR GI, and the RTXGI NRC training and query paths. The related M8 measurements are in [m8_optimization_and_fixes.md](./m8_optimization_and_fixes.md).

The comparison draws on four NVIDIA references. [RTXDI integration guide](https://github.com/NVIDIA-RTX/RTXDI/blob/v3.2.0/Doc/Integration.md), [ReSTIR GI](https://github.com/NVIDIA-RTX/RTXDI/blob/v3.2.0/Doc/RestirGI.md), [Noise and Bias](https://github.com/NVIDIA-RTX/RTXDI/blob/v3.2.0/Doc/NoiseAndBias.md), and the [RTXGI NRC guide](https://github.com/NVIDIA-RTX/RTXGI/blob/v2.9.0/Docs/NrcGuide.md).

## Correctness findings

### High · Reflection rays can cross the geometric surface

The primary GI bounce and the shared scatter used by NRC training and reference paths accept a BRDF sample when its PDF is positive. A normal map can tilt the shading normal enough for a direction to lie above it and below the triangle's geometric normal. The ray-origin offset then moves the ray to the far side of the triangle, where it can collect light from behind the reflecting surface. NVIDIA's GI sample rejects directions with a nonpositive geometric-normal dot product. A rejected primary GI direction should contribute zero radiance and still count as a sample in the reservoir. [Primary bounce](../../shaders/passes/indirect.slang#L53) · [Shared scatter](../../shaders/lighting/path_shading.slang#L98) · [NVIDIA hemisphere check](../../reference/RTXDI/Samples/FullSample/Shaders/LightingPasses/BrdfRayTracing.hlsl#L134)

### High · GI boiling filter reads unwritten reservoir padding

At the usual render width of 853 pixels, the last 8-wide workgroup has three columns of lanes outside the image. The initial pass skips their writes. The temporal pass still loads those lanes' reservoir entries before its bounds check. The entries lie within the block-padded allocation, so the buffer bounds guard cannot distinguish them from written pixels. Their weights can alter the shared boiling threshold for valid edge pixels. Initialize out-of-image lanes with empty reservoirs before the filter and keep those lanes in group synchronization. [Initial write guard](../../shaders/passes/indirect.slang#L144) · [Temporal load and filter](../../shaders/passes/rtxdi_gi_temporal.slang#L34) · [Reservoir allocation](../../src/render/vk_rtxdi.cpp#L92)

### Medium · DI temporal visibility uses geometry from two frames

The DI callback traces from the previous surface toward the previous light using the current TLAS. NVIDIA's fallback for a current-only TLAS starts at the current surface and reconstructs the ray toward the sample position. In the engine the sample stores direction and distance relative to the original surface, so changing the origin alone would leave the ray inconsistent. This can affect ray-traced bias correction around moving geometry. The GI callback already uses the current surface in its fallback. [Engine DI callback](../../shaders/lighting/rtxdi_bridge.slang#L383) · [Engine GI callback](../../shaders/lighting/rtxdi_bridge.slang#L464) · [NVIDIA visibility fallback](../../reference/RTXDI/Samples/FullSample/Shaders/LightingPasses/RtxdiApplicationBridge/RAB_VisibilityTest.hlsli#L68)

### Medium · Sharp GGX peak lost precision

The original audit reproduced a **0.21%** error in both `f × cos θ` and the solid-angle PDF for the off-axis glossy case at roughness `0.05`. The GGX denominator subtracted nearly equal values near the peak. BRDF evaluation and PDF evaluation shared the affected term, so their consistency check passed while both analytic assertions failed. The current working tree evaluates the small term with `cross(n, h)`. The focused `brdf` rerun passed, with the case reporting `f × cos θ = 3.46587` and `PDF = 10.56387`. [GGX evaluation](../../shaders/material/brdf.slang#L36) · [Analytic test case](../../tests/brdf_test.cpp#L258)

## Sampling behavior against NVIDIA's sample

- **ReSTIR DI.** The engine uses initial RIS, temporal reuse, spatial reuse, and final visibility. Uniform local-light selection is valid for the current punctual-light path. NVIDIA's Medium preset uses ReGIR for local candidates, which matters as the light count and scene size grow. The engine's default BRDF candidate spends a ray even though its bridge cannot return a mesh light or environment sample. More light candidates for Sponza's lone delta sun cannot increase the number of indirect rays that reach sunlit geometry. [DI defaults](../../src/render/vk_rtxdi.cpp#L140) · [Light bridge](../../shaders/lighting/rtxdi_bridge.slang#L390) · [M7 scope](../m7/README.md)
- **ReSTIR GI.** The engine stores the secondary hit's position, normal, and outgoing radiance. The inverse of the primary ray's solid-angle PDF initializes the reservoir weight. Final shading applies the reservoir weight and uses NVIDIA's glossy blend with the original sample. The secondary hit retains full material gloss while the reservoir carries a single radiance value across viewing directions. NVIDIA's sample defaults secondary roughness to at least `0.5` to limit sharp, view-dependent contributions. Also, the ray cone at the engine's first secondary hit excludes the bounce distance, while NRC training includes it. Training and query can therefore sample different material mip levels on long bounces. [Final shading](../../shaders/passes/rtxdi_gi_shade.slang#L36) · [NVIDIA roughness default](../../reference/RTXDI/Samples/FullSample/Source/RenderPasses/LightingPasses/LightingPasses.cpp#L49) · [GI hit sampling](../../shaders/passes/indirect.slang#L168) · [NRC hit sampling](../../shaders/passes/nrc_update.slang#L128)
- **RTXGI NRC.** The training path matches NVIDIA's order for recording hits, adding local lighting, and passing the scatter PDF to the SDK. The query path disables the spread heuristic and always queries the first secondary hit. NVIDIA uses the heuristic to trace farther when the path footprint is still small, such as through smooth reflections or in corners. Fixed query placement could contribute to the previously measured **~10% bright bias** in NRC modes. Its contribution remains unmeasured. The default ReSTIR GI mode pauses NRC. [Engine query](../../shaders/passes/indirect.slang#L129) · [NVIDIA termination heuristic](https://github.com/NVIDIA-RTX/RTXGI/blob/v2.9.0/Docs/NrcGuide.md#path-termination-heuristic) · [Recorded NRC bias](../considerations.md#m4--g5-restir-gi)

## Verification

The original release CTest run on the RTX 5070 passed 6 of 7 targets, including `lights` and `rtxdi_math`. The only failing target was `brdf`, which failed the two sharp-glossy assertions. The focused BRDF rerun passed after the working-tree change described above. The geometric-normal, reservoir-padding, and motion-related findings come from source inspection. Their image impact still needs scene-level measurement.
